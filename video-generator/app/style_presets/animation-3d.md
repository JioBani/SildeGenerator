# 3D animated cinema

Render original characters and environments as dimensional animated-film stills.
Use sculpted silhouettes, readable facial planes, tactile material separation,
soft area lighting and controlled depth. Keep character proportions, costume,
material roughness and light direction consistent across connected shots.
Stage visible goals and competing actions through eye lines, hands and blocking.
Vary establishing shots, medium action shots and expressive details by purpose,
not random camera rotation. Avoid flat clip art, photoreal human skin, typography,
logos and generic smiling poses. This is a visual medium, not a promise of an
articulated 3D actor or generated character motion.
