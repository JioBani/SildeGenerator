# Fantasy Anime / 0.1.0 / experimental

Visual style, independent of narrative genre. Set style_bible.preset_id to fantasy_anime.
Use original characters and settings, not a named studio, artist or franchise replica.
Preserve the script: fantasy treatment does not authorize adding magic, characters,
events or false evidence to factual narration.

Design: clean expressive 2D contours, two or three cel-shadow tones on characters,
hand-painted background masses, atmospheric depth, controlled detail at the focal point.
Use a coherent neutral base with complementary warm light and cool environmental accents.
Avoid photorealistic skin, plastic 3D surfaces, inconsistent line thickness and
indiscriminate particles/glow. Keep costume, facial ratios and palette stable.
Document these anchors in the style bible and recurring asset descriptions.

Define foreground, subject plane and background in each visual summary. Use an
establishing view when geography matters; move to reaction/detail when meaning shifts.
Choose a meaningful focal target rather than arbitrary center zoom. Protect the face
and reveal enough environment to understand scale. Keep subtitle safety margins.
No embedded letters, logos, pseudo-text, maps or unverified geographic claims.

This is a slide cinematic treatment: expression stills, composition, camera and editing.
Do not claim fluid character acting from a single image, invent mesh readiness or turn
the whole image into independent moving objects. Identity and actual crop safety need
visual review. Test establishing, close-up and information cuts on another script too.
