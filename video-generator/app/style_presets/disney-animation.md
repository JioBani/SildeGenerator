# Disney-inspired original animation

Use expressive hand-drawn animated storytelling: clean variable linework,
appealing original character silhouettes, readable facial acting, painted
backgrounds, selective cel shading and deliberate warm/cool light contrast.
Lock face shape, costume identifiers, line density and anatomy across shots.
Make tension, hesitation and relief visible in posing rather than exaggerating
every expression. Preserve narrative stakes without forcing a happy mood.
Create original characters and settings, not existing franchise characters,
studio logos, signatures or marketing claims of affiliation. Do not alternate
between live action, glossy 3D, flat icons and drawn animation within a sequence.
