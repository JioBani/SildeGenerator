# Dystopian cinematic storytelling

Use grounded cinematic realism with oppressive architecture, worn surfaces and
spatial barriers that make the subject's limited choices readable. Scale,
negative space and eye lines express power relations. Use practical light,
restrained saturation and one motivated accent, but preserve faces, hands and
important evidence with sufficient local contrast. Darkness is not the goal.
Preserve the selected era and facts: do not invent surveillance technology,
disasters or coercion as historical evidence. Avoid generic neon cyberpunk,
arbitrary ruins, text, logos and repeated lonely silhouettes. Keep identity,
weather, material and screen-direction continuity between connected shots.
