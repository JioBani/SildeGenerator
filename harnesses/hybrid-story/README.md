# hybrid-story 0.18.1 (private development candidate)

## New-script three-route transfer and measured subtitles

`0.18.1` keeps the 0.18.0 camera behavior and rejects malformed explicit
subtitle cues before rendering: overlaps, scene overflow and cues above two
lines. It does not change default subtitles or public slide engines. A new
43.9-second authored night-lights film uses two distinct generated rooftop
stills, an editable three-step composite diagram, and a hash-registered NASA
2016 night-lights map. The map is labeled as a composite, not a single-night
photo or direct proof of star visibility. The first film exposed oversized
full-scene captions; a measured sentence-level narration/caption revision is
the current candidate. This establishes only a technical three-route transfer
and a concrete typography improvement, not autonomous direction, actor
performance or audience understanding. See
`docs/night-lights-hybrid-transfer-0.18.1-20260926.md`.

## Cinematic camera window

Only a cinematic scene with an explicit push/pull gets a camera move. The
measured voice duration is divided into an initial hold, a bounded
`sine.inOut` move and a final settle; under-1.8-second travel fails instead
of becoming a linear or snap zoom. Diagram and source cameras remain fixed.
The selected focus and move window are recorded in `program.json`.

The previous authored mixed-route film was re-rendered with exactly the same
cinematic asset bytes, NASA prepared source bytes, voice and captions. The
candidate, source record and decoded pixel observations are in
`output/hybrid-story-0.18.0-camera-ab/`. Technical pixel checks passed. This
is not a new-script director result, emotional approval or source proof for
the fictional exploration. See
`docs/hybrid-camera-settle-0.18.0-20260926.md`. The 0.17.0 source is preserved
in `output/hybrid-story-0.17.0-baseline`; public engines remain unchanged.

## Preproduction Brief

preproduction-brief-0.1 compiles accepted meaning beats into a provider-free,
hash-bound scene-design brief. Each beat carries its viewer change, information
task, selected route, and the registered grammar's required visual events,
runtime requirements, exclusions and limits. Registered source claims retain
their catalog context; cinematic beats state the intended change and still-image
boundary. The identical brief enters concept selection and scene design before
image or voice work. The selected concept records its brief hash. A mismatch
between concept and scene design, or a changed brief during repair, stops the run.

Core also checks that a cause-change-result diagram actually changes an affected
target after its incoming relation. This is an executable structure check, not
proof of factual causality or visual quality. Existing diagram validators retain
their other grammar-specific checks. See
docs/preproduction-brief-development-20260926.md. Previous 0.14.0 source is
preserved under output/hybrid-story-0.14.0-baseline. Public engines are unchanged.

## Bounded Inferred-Relation Alternatives

When an independent semantic review has only one UNKNOWN inferred motivation
or event-cause link, relation-alternatives-0.1 can compare a context reading with
a sequence reading. The two candidates preserve the same script, beats, routes,
facts and source bindings. A separate critic reviews both orders, every criterion
must agree, and the selected analysis requires a final sufficiency review. These
two alternatives consume two of the shared three planning repairs. Explicit
causal links, multiple findings and unavailable review stop the attempt.

The frozen mixed-script replay reached the first independent comparison, then
the subscription review provider returned 401 on the reverse-order comparison.
No winner, scene plan or film was produced. Existing public engines are unchanged.
See docs/relation-alternatives-development-20260926.md.

## Visible Text Meaning Gate

visible-text-0.2 runs after structurally valid scene design and before returning
the plan for asset work. A separate critic checks exact diagram title/label slots
for completeness, fidelity and referent clarity. Noun labels are valid; unfinished
assertions, dropped negation/uncertainty and invented quantitative claims are not.
No verdict supplies permission to modify narration, values, geometry or sources.

A failure may use one remaining shared planning repair: propose three complete
short text alternatives, reject overlength alternatives without slicing them,
review the shortest eligible proposal independently, and stop if it still fails.
UNKNOWN and provider failures stop immediately. Changed grounded units/referents
require scene re-planning rather than text-only correction. No public release.
Previous0.12.1 and the failed0.13.0 direct-rewrite prototype remain under output/.
See docs/visible-text-gate-development-20260926.md. This is not actual-frame OCR,
whole-film validation, held-out quality proof or viewer-emotion approval.

## Required Grammar Before Feasibility

The opt-in required-grammar-0.1 semantic policy separates what a script needs
from what the runtime supports. Analysis may name an unsupported grammar from

It must not use a null grammar or invent a supported substitute. This fixes an

## Two-Time Measurement Grammar

hybrid-scene-0.5 adds before_after with snapshot-pair-0.1. The same explicitly
named entity and metric appear at two ordered, exactly quoted times in one unit.
The renderer retains the first measurement and reveals the second on an equal
zero-based scale. Increases, decreases and unchanged values are valid. It draws
no causal arrow and fabricates no intermediate values. This first scope does not
cover qualitative shape changes, acting, signed axes or arbitrary time series.

Core checks identity/metric/time/value quote grounding, source order and palette.
The locked director schema exposes the pair rather than generic nodes/edges.
Numeric events can bind to measured local narration. Final pixels are independently
measured; a separate browser font-box check detects clipping and text collision.
Three authored development scripts are not held-out automatic-director evidence.
The previous0.8 harness is preserved at output/hybrid-story-0.8-baseline.

## Meaning Before Design

Private0.4 opts into semantic-beats-0.1. Core runs a separate subscription analysis
request before scene design, creating exact contiguous meaning spans instead of
using sentence boundaries as scene boundaries. Every span declares the viewer
change, purpose, chosen/rejected routes, grammar, research/evidence needs and
confidence. Core owns the span-to-source-unit binding; scene design cannot merge
different beats, replace routes/grammars, or rewrite narration unnoticed.

The analysis and design stages share at most three repair requests. Two consecutive
equivalent failures stop retries. Unsupported grammar, unknown evidence or low
confidence requires further engine/review work, not a generic still fallback.
Low-confidence independent alternative judging is not implemented yet; such input
fails closed instead of inflating its quality status. Source metadata is not fact
verification. This first analysis contract supports episode_60, not long-form merge.

The real create_plan path is tested with explicit offline HTTP MockTransport.
Plain deterministic mock mode is rejected for this opt-in contract. No live model,
paid provider or automatic deployment is part of this development validation.

## Illustrated Transport

The 0.2 scene contract adds optional `composition` to a `transport_track` diagram.
This remains an explanatory beat: a generated background, independently moving
alpha sprite, editable cubic path and endpoint states share one fixed coordinate
system. GSAP MotionPathPlugin from the existing pinned runtime controls orientation
and eased motion; no new runtime installation is used.

Core resolves `asset_pack_id` from `ILLUSTRATION_ASSETS_DIR/catalog.json` (default
`/data/illustration-assets`). This registry is separate from historical sources.
No planner paths or URLs grant file access. Pack roles, hashes, alpha, aspect and
conservative sprite clearance are checked; render rechecks prepared-file hashes.
Empty catalogs expose no illustration packs. An invented ID fails planning review.
No matching plate means no illustrated transport, not reuse of unrelated pictures.

The first capability accepts two endpoints, one monotonic cubic path, a clear
rectangular movement corridor, right-facing top-down art, and fixed camera.
It does not detect land, solve occlusion, generate rigs, simulate boat physics,
adapt landscape art to portrait, or establish historical route correctness.
Safe rectangles are trusted authored metadata and still require visual auditing.
Original 0.1 harness code is retained locally in output/hybrid-story-0.1-baseline.
Both versions are private; the pure slide engine is unchanged.

Benchmark: `scripts/smoke_illustrated_transport.py <new-output-directory>`.
Output critic: `scripts/audit_illustrated_transport.py <video.mp4>` checks decoded
frames for the original coral-launch fixture. Its three negative controls do not
prove the goal's broad held-out/semantic acceptance thresholds.

Implementation reference: https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/MotionPathPlugin/

This candidate routes each scene explicitly through cinematic stills, editable
GSAP diagrams, or registered source media. It forks narrative-slide 0.1.0 and
uses the existing pinned HyperFrames 0.8.46 / GSAP 3.14.2 Docker runtime.
There are no new runtime dependencies and no Core-private imports.

`layout.hybrid` is typed by the public SDK. Diagram routes support causal chains,
common-scale quantitative comparison and endpoint transport. Every node/edge
has normalized timing; final reading hold is reserved. Only hard cuts are
supported. Still-image camera changes do not claim character animation.

Core alone resolves source IDs from SOURCE_MEDIA_DIR/catalog.json, checks the
registered hash, prepares the complete image without crop, and records attribution.
The renderer rechecks the prepared hash. Missing source/diagram work never invokes
an image provider. Only cinematic shots take the normal image broker path.
`prepare_local_scene_asset(scene, output, settings)` is the provider-free diagram
poster hook, used for the normal image cache/thumbnail contract. The poster does
not replace the animated graph in the final video.

Core changes: opt-in director schema/repair, trusted source catalog in planner
context, local asset preparation before provider requests, measured duration gate.
Old harnesses do not receive these additions. Source metadata is not proof that
a historical claim is true; dated modern material must not represent an old event.

Validate: `python scripts/harness.py validate hybrid-story`.
The autonomous goal is not completed by schema acceptance. Real mixed rendering,
negative controls, independent semantic critique and product E2E remain required.

## Inherited narrative contract

Forked from cinematic-slide 0.6.0. The director emits a typed `layout.direction`
for every shot. Character stories expose goals, stakes, competing choices and
consequences. Observational essays retain a visual subject without inventing drama.
The real planning path automatically requests up to two repairs for missing
causes, unexplained state changes or unmotivated repeated shots. Final contract
errors stop asset work. All review attempts remain in the job asset ledger.

Shot size, viewpoint, visible action and focal target reach the image provider.
Camera hold/push/pull and hard cuts execute in the hybrid graph.
The automatic contract gate has no human-review dependency. It does not measure
rendered acting, emotional response or aesthetic quality; those evaluation layers
remain on the development plan. Hybrid routing is implemented in this candidate.

Validate with `python scripts/harness.py validate hybrid-story` and
`python -m unittest discover -s video-generator/tests -p test_narrative_direction.py`
with `video-generator` on PYTHONPATH. This candidate is not yet a public option.

Inherited rendering behavior: scene layout.camera specifies hold/push/pull,
focus [x,y] normalized in the final crop, and strength 0..0.08. Default is hold.
Explicit continuity_group requires hold on every member, including across segments.
Invalid camera intent is rejected, not silently replaced. Image geometry still needs
visual review. No rig, parallax or automatic acting approval is provided.
Public release awaits full-script, visual and service testing.

Within a rendered segment only cut is supported. Other transitions fail preflight.
Segment assembly concatenates segments. The episode's 10-scene/60-second limits
remain in effect. Inherited transition helpers do not expand this route contract.

Cinematic frames display an AI-illustration label. Registered source frames show
date and attribution; prepared files are rehashed by the renderer. A modern source
remains modern even in a historical script. This is not historical fact checking.
