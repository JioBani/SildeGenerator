## Different directing systems

Posture maintains an actor across everyday contexts, then turns the body into an
anatomical explanation and returns to practical examples. Its main reconstruction
challenge is contact: seat/body, hand/phone, feet/floor, adult/child and body/bed.
Actor identity must survive changes of pose, scale and abstraction.

Blood pressure maintains recurring body, vessel and particle roles across changes
of scale. It makes otherwise hidden processes visible through cross-sections,
flow, wall deformation, anchored labels and staged intervention. Its main challenge
is preserving object state and causal order as the camera and representation change.

These are observed patterns and hypotheses. They are not universal filmmaking rules
or permission to automatically approve a renderer or actor capability.

## Dense-window findings

### Posture seating, 162-192 seconds

The seat initially supports the pelvis while the legs are extended. The legs lower,
then the desk context appears. A close-up exposes the eye-monitor relation; another
exposes the forearm, armrest and desk relationship. The desk appears to adjust relative
to the hand in the sampled sequence. Before reproducing this, measure its trajectory
and contact timing from the native frames rather than assuming the arm moves instead.

Necessary invariants: pelvis-seat contact; consistent chair position; feet settle
without sliding; hand and desk relationship preserved; eye line uses the actual
monitor target. Review frame boundaries and occlusions separately from pose appeal.

### Blood intervention, 201-231 seconds

Particles clear from the focus area; a thin device enters from the left, a rounded
component grows at the narrowed region, contracts and is followed by a mesh-like
device. Flow returns while the retained structure stays located in the lumen.
This describes visible source graphics, not clinical instructions or verified anatomy.

Necessary invariants: device follows the lumen; inflation remains attached to its
carrier; wall/deposit shape reacts without an unexplained jump; retained structure
does not travel away with withdrawing equipment; returning particles do not cross
the solid wall. The ability to draw or scale an ellipse alone cannot pass this scene.

## Machine-readable output

verifies source duration, hashes source and evidence, and emits research scene records
with scene function, design, layout, object states, motion hypothesis, unresolved
transition/rhythm checks and required renderer capabilities. It also emits a reverse
index of capability labels to affected scenes.

There are 77 capability labels, not 77 missing libraries or approved engine features.
The names need consolidation against actual verified assets before implementation.
All emitted records explicitly prohibit automatic rendering/promotion and keep
measured trajectories empty until evidence exists. Existing production defaults are
unchanged. Five validation tests reject gaps, missing tails and duplicate references,
and prove that compiling observations does not grant approval.
