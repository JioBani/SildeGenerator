# Independent Slide Engine

Status: private development, narrative-slide@0.1.0. Not a hybrid-engine mode.

## Output

Designed cinematic stills, purposeful framing/cuts, restrained camera motion,
narration and editable typography/subtitles. Existing classic-slide releases are
preserved. Do not insert explanatory motion-graphics scenes behind the user's
slide selection. Do not switch a slide job to hybrid automatically.

## Work

- Analyse semantic beats, visible actions, goals/obstacles, dilemmas actually
  present in the script, causal consequences and state continuity.
- Improve shot size/viewpoint selection, meaningful callbacks, dramatic cut timing,
  and focal-target camera motion with settle/reading time.
- Develop the four styles independently of the hybrid engine: live-action cinema,
  3D animation, original family storybook animation, dystopia.
- Preserve original narration and meaning. A slide that cannot adequately explain
  a mechanism must report the limitation, not silently render a diagram route.
- Use an independent frame critic and held-out slide scripts, measuring framing,
  action/meaning fit, repetition, identity, typography, voice timing and camera.
- Compare slide baseline against slide candidates. Hybrid benchmarks/approvals
  never establish slide quality. No hybrid source-route requirement is imposed
  on a pure-slide acceptance report.

## Boundary

Planning contract engine_family=slide. layout.hybrid is rejected before assets.
Own prompts/config/render package and release snapshot. Shared SDK contracts,
brokers, fonts, evidence storage and reusable evaluators are infrastructure only.
No dependency on the hybrid renderer or its visual-grammar library.

## Completion

New held-out scripts render different appropriate cinematic shots in all four
styles, with no critical semantic/cut/typographic/camera regression. Independent
automatic comparison and scoped repair must run without routine human review.
Production deployment remains a separate explicitly authorized operation.
