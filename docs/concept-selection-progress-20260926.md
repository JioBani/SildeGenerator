## Acceptance Target

The user requires comparable quality on DIFFERENT scripts, and appropriate
visualization of diverse information. Replaying the same frames with new speech,
changing only colors, or forcing every explanation into one diagram is failure.
Development examples used to tune the engine cannot later count as held-out data.

Five executable grammar families exist: cause/change/result, quantitative
comparison, transport, cumulative change, and ordered procedure. This is not yet
coverage of whole/detail, before/after, decomposition/recombination,
evidence/judgment or general map/spatial reasoning. Missing families must not be
silently replaced with still zooms or superficially similar supported diagrams.
